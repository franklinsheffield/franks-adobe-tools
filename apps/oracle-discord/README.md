# Science is Magic oracle bot

One Discord app for the server's readings: `/oracle` (the fortune teller), `/humandesign`, `/astrology`, `/iching`, `/numerology`, `/chinesezodiac`, `/mayan`, `/egyptian`, `/celtic`, `/runes`, `/oraclebones`, `/ifa`, plus `/profile`.

- **Worker** (`worker/index.js`, Cloudflare free tier): checks Discord's signature, defers the reply, seals the options with AES-GCM (`ORACLE_KEY`) and dispatches `.github/workflows/oracle-reading.yml`.
- **Reading job** (`scripts/job.js`, GitHub Actions): opens the options, runs `src/modules/<name>/index.js`, posts embeds + PNG to Discord. Birth data is never stored or logged.
- **Modules**: export `command` (slash command JSON) and `async run(options, ctx)` -> `{ embeds, files: [{ name, data }], ephemeral? }`. List them in `src/registry.js`.
- **Shared core**: `src/ephemeris.js` (astronomy-engine, MIT; tests check it against Swiss Ephemeris to under 1 arcminute) and `src/birthplace.js` (OpenStreetMap geocoding, IANA time zone, historical DST).

## The Oracle (`/oracle`)
`src/oracle/`: the Science is Magic fortune teller. `/oracle` opens her card (only the visitor sees it): a portrait, an in-character greeting and a menu of every registered system (`systems.js`; new modules appear once they're in `MODULES`).
- **Question systems** (I Ching, runes, oracle bones, Ifá) open a question form; **birth systems** use the saved `/profile` or open the birth form.
- The reading posts as usual, opened by the Oracle's spoken lines (`voice.js`, written text, no AI service) with her name on the first embed.
- Every reading ends with **Another reading** and **Enter the Chamber** buttons. The Chamber button launches the Oracle Chamber Activity once `ACTIVITY_READY = "true"` in `wrangler.toml`.
- Portrait: `figure.js` draws her; `node scripts/make-portrait.js` re-renders `assets/oracle.png` and the copy the Worker serves at `/oracle.png`.

## The Oracle Chamber (Discord Activity)
`activity/`: the full-screen fortune-teller experience. The Oracle (the creature from the server logo, `src/oracle/figure.js`) sits top left, the reading table top right, and its words scroll in the bottom half. Pick a system, give the Oracle your question or birth details; the tools are cast on the table with an animation (`activity/src/table.js`), the reading art is laid down, and the Oracle talks it through with retro text-box blips (`activity/src/voice-sfx.js`, Web Audio, mute button). Also a journal of your last 20 readings (on that device) and your saved `/profile` filled in.
- Readings run in the browser with the same modules as the bot (`build.mjs` bundles every module in `MODULES`, swapping resvg, `node:fs` and `node:url` for shims in `activity/src/shims/`), so they always match the chat version.
- `activity/worker.mjs` (Worker `oracle-chamber`): serves the build, swaps the Discord sign-in code for a token (`DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`, from repo secret `ORACLE_DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`) and returns the member's own saved profile.
- Discord Developer Portal: Activities on, URL mapping `/` -> the `oracle-chamber` Worker host and `/osm` -> `nominatim.openstreetmap.org`, OAuth2 redirect `https://127.0.0.1`. Then set `ACTIVITY_READY = "true"` in `wrangler.toml` so the bot's **Enter the Chamber** button launches it.
- Local: `node activity/build.mjs`, then serve `activity/dist/` and open it in a browser (sign-in and profile are skipped outside Discord).

### Rooms: Séance: Guess the Ghost

The Chamber hosts rooms, small games listed under "Investigate & learn" (registry `ROOMS` in `activity/src/main.js`; each room lives in `activity/src/<room>/` and keeps progress in `prog.rooms.<id>` with a `plays` counter). **Séance: Guess the Ghost** (`activity/src/seance/`): a ghost raps out 5 clues, each one real method, tool or fact of what it practised. Three levels: **1 Apprentice**, 36 practitioners (a cardiologist, a card sharp, a horoscope columnist…), named by their general kind from a 12-answer pool (astronomy, physics, chemistry, biology, geology, stage illusion, sleight of hand, escapology, mentalism, spirit mediumship, alchemy, astrology); some count as two (a pharmacist is chemistry or biology), and typing the job title also counts; **2 Adept**, 20 specific techniques (spectroscopy, radiocarbon dating, tree-ring dating, X-ray crystallography, pasteurisation, Zener cards, bullet catch, Zig Zag Girl, linking rings, distillation…); **3 Master**, 20 historical scientists and magicians (Tesla, Newton, Dee, Pepper, Kepler and Paracelsus count as both). Say magic, science or both, then name it: type it (typos forgiven), or "show me 4", which blows out 3 candles (always leaving one). Five candles; extra clues and wrong names snuff one. No `<form>`: Discord's Activity sandbox blocks form submit, so the answer box uses a button and Enter. Points scale ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2 by level; first identification pays ✦ and 💎, replays only improvements. Decks in `practices.js` (POOL, GENERAL, SPECIFIC) and `ghosts.js`. Store shelf (`shop.js`, kind `seancecandle`): séance candles coloured by real flame tests (lithium crimson, copper green, calcium orange, potassium lilac, will-o'-the-wisp blue), equipped id in `prog.seancecandle`.

### Rooms: Rune & Glyph Decoder

**Rune & Glyph Decoder** (`activity/src/decoder/`) teaches four real scripts in 19 levels: Elder Futhark (the futhark, the bracteate words alu and laþu, the Einang Stone, the Gallehus horn), Ogham (letter names, OGAM, the MAQI MUCOI formula), Egyptian hieroglyphs (rn, the cartouches of Ptolemy, Cleopatra and Tutankhamun) and Maya numbers (bar and dot, 13.0.0.0.0, Pakal's birth 9.8.9.13.0). The Oracle teaches each new sign, the inscription is laid on the table, and the player taps the sound of each glyph, then answers what it means and gets its history and source. Each script also has a make-your-own level: your name in runes, Ogham or a cartouche, or your birthday in the Long Count (from `src/modules/mayan/calendar.js`). Stars: ★★★ from memory, ★★ with the codebook, ★ with hints. Each new star earns 5 ✦ and 5 💎, plus 5 more for a first clear. Codex entries appear under "Decoded". Each script has a 📖 Study guide (`guide.js`) that covers everything its questions ask, plus a full sign table. It opens from the script menu, the lesson and the meaning question, and closes while decoding (there the codebook costs a star). Glyph fonts are OFL Noto subsets embedded in `fonts.gen.js`. Decoder levels don't use daily readings. The Essence store has a 🗝️ Decoder tablets shelf (`shop.js`, registered through `registerShelf`): 8 surfaces every inscription is laid on (Starlit slate free; birch board 60, red-ochre runestone 90, standing stone 90, papyrus 100, lapis tomb wall 120, jade stela 120, gold bracteate 150 💎). Try-on lays a sample inscription on the table; the choice is `prog.tablet`.

### Rooms: Sacred Geometry Builder

**Sacred Geometry Builder** (`activity/src/geometry/`) is drawn with Euclid's two tools only: tap 🧭 Compass (centre, then a point it passes through) or 📏 Straightedge (two points). Lines and circles can only start on points that exist: given points and crossings, so every figure is a real construction (`engine.js`, pure and tested). 12 levels in three tiers: **1 Apprentice** (the next move glows): circle and radius, vesica piscis (√3), Euclid I.1 equilateral triangle, hexagon, Seed of Life; **2 Adept** (written steps, 💡 Show me costs a star): perpendicular bisector, square in a circle, the golden section φ, and the five Platonic solids (turning wireframes; count the faces, then Euler's V − E + F = 2); **3 Master** (goal only): Ptolemy's pentagon, the 19-circle Flower of Life, the cube in Metatron's Cube. Each figure ends with a question, the history (myths flagged, e.g. φ and the Parthenon) and a source. Stars: ★★★ no hints and within par + 2 moves, ★★ up to 2 hints, ★ more; 5 ✦ + 5 💎 per new star plus 5 for a first clear; Codex entries and titles (Apprentice of the Compass → Keeper of the Golden Ratio). 📖 Study guide (`guide.js`) covers every question. Store shelf 📐 Geometer's sets (`shop.js`, `prog.geo`): 8 board-and-ink styles with a try-on preview.
### Rooms: Calendar Keeper

**Calendar Keeper** (`activity/src/calendar/`) turns the reading table into the Keeper's desk: the Maya Tzolk'in gears (13 numbers meshed with 20 day signs), the Egyptian 365-day civil year and the Celtic wheel of the year, all turning to the same day, with the Haab', Long Count, Coptic date, next fire festival, Sun position and Moon phase. Three levels, 26 puzzles: 1 Apprentice (how the cycles turn), 2 Daykeeper (predict the festivals: Maya New Year, Wepet Renpet, Nayrouz, Samhain eve, astronomical cross-quarter days) and 3 Sky Watcher (predict eclipses with the Saros, eclipse seasons, the Dresden Codex table, the exeligmos). Questions are generated fresh each play and every answer is computed: Maya dates from `src/modules/mayan/calendar.js` (GMT 584283), the Egyptian civil year from the Era of Nabonassar (checked against Censorinus), Coptic from `src/modules/egyptian`, eclipses and solar longitudes live from astronomy-engine. Stars: 3 per puzzle, each hint or wrong answer costs one; each new star pays 4 ✦ and 3 💎 times the level's multiplier (×1, ×1.5, ×2), plus 5 ✦ on a first solve. Level 2 opens after 4 Level 1 solves, Level 3 after 4 Level 2 solves. A free Keeper's desk turns to any date and jumps to the next festival or eclipse. Four study guides (Maya, Egyptian, Celtic, eclipses) in `guide.js`. Store shelf "🗓️ Calendar Keeper" sells desk looks (prog.calendar). No daily readings used. Tests: `tests/calendar.test.js`.

## Birth-data systems (milestone 1 of the 8-system expansion)
All use `/profile` (now with an optional birth name) and the pop-up form, which asks only for what each system needs (`BIRTH_FIELDS` in `src/profiles.js`). Shared card renderer: `src/card.js`.
- `/numerology`: Pythagorean life path, expression, soul urge, personality, birthday, personal year/month, maturity, pinnacles and challenges, karmic debt; masters 11/22/33 kept.
- `/chinesezodiac`: animal/element/yin-yang by the real lunar new year (new moons and principal terms from astronomy-engine, UTC+8), four pillars from solar terms (year at Lichun, day at 23:00), element balance, trines/clash, this year.
- `/mayan`: Tzolk'in day sign + tone, kin, trecena, Haab', Long Count, Lord of the Night (GMT 584283), bar-and-dot numerals.
- `/egyptian`: the modern "Egyptian zodiac" deity plus the birthday in the Coptic (Egyptian) calendar. Labelled as a modern reconstruction.
- `/celtic`: Graves' tree calendar with ogham letter and fire-festival season. Labelled as a modern reconstruction.

## Cast systems (milestone 2)
- `/runes`: 24 Elder Futhark runes drawn without replacement; one, three (Norns) or five (cross) spreads; reversible runes land merkstave half the time.
- `/oraclebones`: a charge (or the Shang routine ten-day charge), paired with its opposite, cracked on turtle plastron or ox scapula; Shang crack notations; dated with today's real day in the 60-day cycle; labelled as a modern reflection in the Shang format.

### Rooms: History of Magic

**History of Magic** (`activity/src/timeline/`) is a timeline game: the Oracle deals real, datable events from the history of magic, stage conjuring, science and skepticism (65 cards across four eras: the Ancient World, Middle Ages & Renaissance, Age of Reason & Wonders, the Modern Age), and the player taps the gap on the timeline where each one belongs. The first card is laid down free; each wrong place empties one of 3 hourglasses and the card is filed where it really goes, with its date and story. Three levels: **1 Apprentice**, 5 cards across all history; **2 Adept**, 6 cards from an era and its neighbour; **3 Master**, 7 cards from one era, some only a few years apart. Cards are only dealt together when their dates, including the uncertainty of ancient ones, can't overlap (`events.js` `pm`), so every order is certain. Points 10 / 15 / 20 per card, plus leftover hourglasses and a perfect-game bonus. Each card placed right for the first time enters your Chronicle (Codex section by era) and pays 4 ✦ and 4 💎; a new best pays the improvement in ✦; a first perfect game at a level pays 10 / 20 / 30 💎. Titles: Time Tourist, Apprentice Chronicler, Keeper of Years, Royal Historian, Master of the Chronicle. Each era has a 📖 study guide (`guide.js`) covering every card in it. Store shelf ⏳ Timeline (`shop.js`): Parchment scroll (free), Astronomer's star chart, Inventor's blueprint, Illuminated manuscript, Time-machine neon. Doesn't use daily readings.

### Rooms: Alchemist's Lab

**Alchemist's Lab** (`activity/src/alchemy/`): mix real historical alchemy and learn the chemistry behind it. Three levels, 31 works: **1 Apprentice**, read the classic symbols (14: the seven planetary metals, sulfur, salt, the four elements, antimony); **2 Journeyman**, follow real recipes at the furnace (10: vermilion, lead calx, aqua fortis, aqua regia, dissolving gold, iron into "copper", sugar of lead, spirit of salt, aqua vitae, the Tree of Diana); **3 Adept**, repeat the experiments that made chemistry (7: Brand's phosphorus, Black's fixed air, Cavendish's hydrogen, Priestley's oxygen, Lavoisier's 12-day mercury experiment, Scheele's chlorine, Davy's potassium), then name what was really found. On the bench, pick up to 3 reagents and an operation (heat, dissolve, distil, sublime; level 3 adds electrolyse) and Transmute; feedback counts right, extra and missing reagents. Four furnace flames: a wrong answer, a failed mix or a notebook hint puts one out. Every result shows the history, the modern chemistry and a balanced equation (tests check every equation balances), and lights its elements on **My periodic table** (23 elements in their real places). Points ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2; first success pays ✦ and 💎, replays only improvements. Titles Puffer, Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Chymist. 📓 **Alchemist's notebook** (`guide.js`) is the study guide: history, elements and principles, metals and planets, operations, old names vs modern chemicals, alchemy to chemistry, safety. 🏺 **Apothecary**: 13 cosmetic bench items (vessels, flame-test coloured fires, wall charms) sold in-room and on the "⚗️ Alchemist's Lab" shelf of the Essence store (`shop.js`, registered with `registerShelf`). Content in `lab.js`, rules in `logic.js`, drawing in `scene.js`.
