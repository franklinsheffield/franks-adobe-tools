# Science is Magic oracle bot

One Discord app for the server's readings: `/oracle` (the fortune teller), `/humandesign`, `/astrology`, `/iching`, `/numerology`, `/chinesezodiac`, `/mayan`, `/egyptian`, `/celtic`, `/runes`, `/oraclebones`, `/ifa`, plus `/profile`.

- **Worker** (`worker/index.js`, Cloudflare free tier): checks Discord's signature, defers the reply, seals the options with AES-GCM (`ORACLE_KEY`) and dispatches `.github/workflows/oracle-reading.yml`.
- **Reading job** (`scripts/job.js`, GitHub Actions): opens the options, runs `src/modules/<name>/index.js`, posts embeds + PNG to Discord. Birth data is never stored or logged.
- **Modules**: export `command` (slash command JSON) and `async run(options, ctx)` -> `{ embeds, files: [{ name, data }], ephemeral? }`. List them in `src/registry.js`.
- **Shared core**: `src/ephemeris.js` (astronomy-engine, MIT; tests check it against Swiss Ephemeris to under 1 arcminute) and `src/birthplace.js` (OpenStreetMap geocoding, IANA time zone, historical DST).

## The Oracle (`/oracle`)
`src/oracle/`: the Science is Magic fortune teller. `/oracle` opens her card (only the visitor sees it): a portrait, an in-character greeting and a menu of every registered system (`systems.js`; new modules appear once they're in `MODULES`).
- **Question systems** (I Ching, runes, oracle bones, Ifá) open a question form; **birth systems** use the saved `/profile` or open the birth form.
- The reading posts as usual, opened by the Oracle's spoken lines (`voice.js`, written text, no AI service) with her name on the first embed.
- Every reading ends with **Another reading** and **Enter the Chamber** buttons. The Chamber button launches the Oracle Chamber Activity once `ACTIVITY_READY = "true"` in `wrangler.toml`.
- Portrait: `figure.js` draws her; `node scripts/make-portrait.js` re-renders `assets/oracle.png` and the copy the Worker serves at `/oracle.png`.

## The Oracle Chamber (Discord Activity)
`activity/`: the full-screen fortune-teller experience. The Oracle (the creature from the server logo, `src/oracle/figure.js`) sits top left, the reading table top right, and its words scroll in the bottom half. Pick a system, give the Oracle your question or birth details; the tools are cast on the table with an animation (`activity/src/table.js`), the reading art is laid down, and the Oracle talks it through with retro text-box blips (`activity/src/voice-sfx.js`, Web Audio, mute button). Also a journal of your last 20 readings (on that device) and your saved `/profile` filled in.
- Readings run in the browser with the same modules as the bot (`build.mjs` bundles every module in `MODULES`, swapping resvg, `node:fs` and `node:url` for shims in `activity/src/shims/`), so they always match the chat version.
- `activity/worker.mjs` (Worker `oracle-chamber`): serves the build, swaps the Discord sign-in code for a token (`DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`, from repo secret `ORACLE_DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`) and returns the member's own saved profile.
- Discord Developer Portal: Activities on, URL mapping `/` -> the `oracle-chamber` Worker host and `/osm` -> `nominatim.openstreetmap.org`, OAuth2 redirect `https://127.0.0.1`. Then set `ACTIVITY_READY = "true"` in `wrangler.toml` so the bot's **Enter the Chamber** button launches it.
- Local: `node activity/build.mjs`, then serve `activity/dist/` and open it in a browser (sign-in and profile are skipped outside Discord).

### Rooms: Séance: Guess the Ghost

The Chamber hosts rooms, small games listed under "Investigate & learn" (registry `ROOMS` in `activity/src/main.js`; each room lives in `activity/src/<room>/` and keeps progress in `prog.rooms.<id>` with a `plays` counter). **Séance: Guess the Ghost** (`activity/src/seance/`): a ghost raps out 5 clues, each one real method, tool or fact of what it practised. Three levels: **1 Apprentice**, 36 practitioners (a cardiologist, a card sharp, a horoscope columnist…), named by their general kind from a 12-answer pool (astronomy, physics, chemistry, biology, geology, stage illusion, sleight of hand, escapology, mentalism, spirit mediumship, alchemy, astrology); some count as two (a pharmacist is chemistry or biology), and typing the job title also counts; **2 Adept**, 20 specific techniques (spectroscopy, radiocarbon dating, tree-ring dating, X-ray crystallography, pasteurisation, Zener cards, bullet catch, Zig Zag Girl, linking rings, distillation…); **3 Master**, 20 historical scientists and magicians (Tesla, Newton, Dee, Pepper, Kepler and Paracelsus count as both). Say magic, science or both, then name it: type it (typos forgiven), or "show me 4", which blows out 3 candles (always leaving one). Five candles; extra clues and wrong names snuff one. No `<form>`: Discord's Activity sandbox blocks form submit, so the answer box uses a button and Enter. Points scale ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2 by level; first identification pays ✦ and 💎, replays only improvements. Decks in `practices.js` (POOL, GENERAL, SPECIFIC) and `ghosts.js`. Store shelf (`shop.js`, kind `seancecandle`): séance candles coloured by real flame tests (lithium crimson, copper green, calcium orange, potassium lilac, will-o'-the-wisp blue), equipped id in `prog.seancecandle`. Sound and animation (`sfx.js`, Web Audio, follows the voice mute): the ghost rises with a chord and knocks, extra clues knock and jolt the table, snuffed candles blow out with smoke, wrong answers get a head-shake and a sour slide, right answers get bells, a wail and sparkles, and running out of candles brings a falling drone.

### Rooms: Rune & Glyph Decoder

**Rune & Glyph Decoder** (`activity/src/decoder/`) teaches four real scripts in 19 levels: Elder Futhark (the futhark, the bracteate words alu and laþu, the Einang Stone, the Gallehus horn), Ogham (letter names, OGAM, the MAQI MUCOI formula), Egyptian hieroglyphs (rn, the cartouches of Ptolemy, Cleopatra and Tutankhamun) and Maya numbers (bar and dot, 13.0.0.0.0, Pakal's birth 9.8.9.13.0). The Oracle teaches each new sign, the inscription is laid on the table, and the player taps the sound of each glyph, then answers what it means and gets its history and source. Each script also has a make-your-own level: your name in runes, Ogham or a cartouche, or your birthday in the Long Count (from `src/modules/mayan/calendar.js`). Stars: ★★★ from memory, ★★ with the codebook, ★ with hints. Each new star earns 5 ✦ and 5 💎, plus 5 more for a first clear. Codex entries appear under "Decoded". Each script has a 📖 Study guide (`guide.js`) that covers everything its questions ask, plus a full sign table. It opens from the script menu, the lesson and the meaning question, and closes while decoding (there the codebook costs a star). Glyph fonts are OFL Noto subsets embedded in `fonts.gen.js`. Decoder levels don't use daily readings. Sound and motion (`sfx.js`, Web Audio, follows the Chamber mute): each script has its own carving sound (chisel, stone knock, reed pen, clay tap) on a rising scale, an opening motif, a wrong-sign thud, a solve run and star pings; glyphs carve in with a glow, the solved line waves and shines, stars pop, sparks burst, script medals float (all off for reduced motion). The Essence store has a 🗝️ Decoder tablets shelf (`shop.js`, registered through `registerShelf`): 8 surfaces every inscription is laid on (Starlit slate free; birch board 60, red-ochre runestone 90, standing stone 90, papyrus 100, lapis tomb wall 120, jade stela 120, gold bracteate 150 💎). Try-on lays a sample inscription on the table; the choice is `prog.tablet`.

### Rooms: Herbalist's Grimoire

**Herbalist's Grimoire** (`activity/src/grimoire/`) is a plant-identification room. A pressed specimen (drawn from data by `sketch.js`) is laid on the table and the Oracle reads 5 true clues: how it looks, where it grows, its folklore, its history, its chemistry. Name it (typed scores double; "show me 4" halves it; 5 leaves: extra clues and wrong typed names cost one, a wrong pick from four costs 3 and the lost leaf falls off the page), then say whether it is 🌿 gentle, ⚠️ handle with care or ☠️ poison, then judge one claim: science or folklore? Each plant earns a grimoire page: folklore, what science says (with an evidence badge), and a safety note; poison pages carry poison-centre advice, and every page says it is not medical advice. Three levels (76 plants in `plants.js` and `plants2.js`): **1 Kitchen garden** (26, incl. daffodil, lily of the valley and nutmeg), **2 Apothecary's meadow** (24), **3 The poison garden** (26). Points ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2; first identification pays ✦ and 💎, replays only improvements; titles Seedling to Master Herbalist; Codex rows "Grimoire: <level>". Each level has a 📖 study guide (`guide.js`). Sounds (`sfx.js`, Web Audio, follows the mute button) and animations: page turn, swaying specimen, falling leaves, sparkles, POISON stamp. Store shelf 🌿 Herbalist (`shop.js`): 7 grimoire page styles, parchment free.

### Rooms: Calendar Keeper

**Calendar Keeper** (`activity/src/calendar/`) turns the reading table into the Keeper's desk: the Maya Tzolk'in gears (13 numbers meshed with 20 day signs), the Egyptian 365-day civil year and the Celtic wheel of the year, all turning to the same day, with the Haab', Long Count, Coptic date, next fire festival, Sun position and Moon phase. Three levels, 26 puzzles: 1 Apprentice (how the cycles turn), 2 Daykeeper (predict the festivals: Maya New Year, Wepet Renpet, Nayrouz, Samhain eve, astronomical cross-quarter days) and 3 Sky Watcher (predict eclipses with the Saros, eclipse seasons, the Dresden Codex table, the exeligmos). Questions are generated fresh each play and every answer is computed: Maya dates from `src/modules/mayan/calendar.js` (GMT 584283), the Egyptian civil year from the Era of Nabonassar (checked against Censorinus), Coptic from `src/modules/egyptian`, eclipses and solar longitudes live from astronomy-engine. Stars: 3 per puzzle, each hint or wrong answer costs one; each new star pays 4 ✦ and 3 💎 times the level's multiplier (×1, ×1.5, ×2), plus 5 ✦ on a first solve. Level 2 opens after 4 Level 1 solves, Level 3 after 4 Level 2 solves. A free Keeper's desk turns to any date and jumps to the next festival or eclipse. Four study guides (Maya, Egyptian, Celtic, eclipses) in `guide.js`. Store shelf "🗓️ Calendar Keeper" sells desk looks (prog.calendar). No daily readings used. Animation and sound (`sfx.js`, Web Audio, follows the Chamber's mute): dials pop in, gears ratchet day by day with clicks and lock with a clunk, long jumps whirl, the gears' window pulses, wrong answers shake the desk with a grind, right answers burst with bells, eclipse answers play a solar or lunar eclipse with a gong, festival answers light bonfires with crackle, stars pop with sparkles, titles get a fanfare, guides turn a page. Tests: `tests/calendar.test.js`.

### Rooms: Element Duel

**Element Duel** (`activity/src/duel/`): a 1-player card battle against the Oracle with the periodic table. Each side has 20 Stability and a hand of 7 element cards (one atom each, showing atomic number and usual oxidation states), dealt from its own shuffled deck weighted toward the abundant elements. Put cards on the bench and **React**: if the bench is a real compound (any order), its power hits the Oracle. Power = atoms + √(−ΔHf°)/8, from each compound's real standard enthalpy of formation; endothermic compounds (NO, NO₂, N₂O, HI, acetylene, ethylene, CS₂, hydrazine) cost the maker 1 Stability unless the lab has High heat or a Spark. A lone noble gas can be raised as an inert shield (blocks 4, free once a turn). **Recycle** discards bench cards and draws (ends the turn). Win: Oracle to 0 Stability, or first to 5 different compounds (30-round cap). Each round draws a random **lab condition** (High heat, Catalyst, Water bath, Oxygen-rich, Argon blanket, Electric spark, Cold trap, Vacuum: each a real effect simplified into a rule) and a random ★ **bonus compound** (+2 power, +3 💎). Levels: **1 Apprentice** 20 elements, 25 two-atom compounds, valid cards glow and a Hint button, free fizzles, recycle 7, novice Oracle; **2 Chemist** 40 elements, +63 multi-atom formulas, no hints, a fizzle costs 1 and explains the charge balance and the real formula, recycle 3, sharper Oracle holding 8 cards; **3 Master** 52 elements, +32 compounds, 8 real **reaction chains** (Contact, Ostwald, lime cycle, Solvay, blast furnace, Kroll, salmiak smoke, methanol: +2 power per consecutive step), no undo, fizzles cost 2 and lose the bench, recycle 2, an Oracle holding 9 cards that plays the best net move and races discoveries (a perfect greedy player wins about 88% / 52% / 42% across the three levels). 120 compounds in all (`compounds.js`, ΔHf° from CRC tables; tests check formulas use only the level's elements, fit a hand, balance with the cards' oxidation states, and spot-check ΔHf° values). Every compound made opens its page (ΔHf°, phase, acid/base/catalyst tags, a true fact) and goes into **My compounds** / the Chamber Codex. Rewards ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2: a win pays ✦ and 💎, plus 2 💎 per new Codex compound, 3 per bonus compound, 10 per new chain. Titles Lab Assistant → Mendeleev's Heir. Store shelf "⚛️ Element Duel" (`shop.js`, `prog.duel`): 6 card backs and 6 lab benches, also sold in-room under 🧪 Lab supplies. Animation and sound (`scene.js`, `style.js`, `sfx.js`, Web Audio, follows the Chamber's mute): cards deal in, the condition card flips each round, atoms fly in and fuse into a ball-and-stick molecule with a flash and boom sized by its power, damage flies at the target bar and shakes it, shields shimmer, fizzles wobble and smoke, victory confetti. The Oracle's turn is automatic with in-character lines. Rules in `logic.js` (pure, seedable). Tests: `tests/duel.test.js` (includes 360 simulated games: every level finishes, hands are playable, Apprentice is easiest).

### Rooms: Star Lore Navigator

**Star Lore Navigator** (`activity/src/stars/`): the Oracle asks the player to **find and draw** a constellation. The sky (a canvas in the table pane) starts at least 60° away from the target, so the player roams the whole sky (drag to pan, pinch / wheel / + − to zoom), finds it, then draws its stick figure by dragging from star to star (or tapping two stars). As each line goes in, the Oracle tells the next part of a fable picked from a pool (37 fables from Greek, Chinese, Egyptian, Lakota, Mi'kmaq, Hindu, Babylonian, Māori, Aboriginal Australian, Inca, Hawaiian and other traditions; unheard fables first). Then the real astronomy, the best evening month and where on Earth it rises (computed from its position), and one quiz question. 16 constellations × 3 levels: **1 Stargazer**, all 88 IAU figures drawn on the sky; **2 Star Joiner**, no figures; **3 Sky Keeper**, photo-real sky with the Milky Way and stars to magnitude 6. A line not in the figure, or a hint, is a mistake; 0-1 mistakes = 3 stars, 2-4 = 2, more = 1. Score 10 per star + 5 for the quiz, ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2 by level; first finds pay ✦ and 💎, replays only improvements. 📖 Study guide (`guide.js`). Store shelf "🔭 Star Lore" (8 sky themes, `prog.stars`). Star data `sky.gen.js` (bright stars, figure edges by HIP, faint stars, Milky Way outline) is generated by `scripts/gen-sky.mjs` from d3-celestial (BSD-3-Clause) with constellation membership from astronomy-engine.

**Constellation Rummy** (`activity/src/rummy/`, prototype): a rummy card game against the Oracle with 48 real stars, 4 from each of 12 constellations (positions, magnitudes and colours from the Star Lore sky data; myths shared with Star Lore Navigator's `lore.js`). Each turn: draw (deck or top discard), lay down sets or add to sets on the table, discard one; empty your hand to go out. Sets: 3-4 stars of one constellation; from Level 2 also 3+ stars of one spectral class (B, A, F, K, M), each from a different constellation. Cards score by brightness (★★★ magnitude below 1, ★★ 1 to 2, ★ fainter); score = laid down minus left in hand, +5 for going out. Levels: 1 Stargazer (6 constellations, 6 cards), 2 Star Hopper (9, 7 cards, colour sets), 3 Sky Keeper (12, 8 cards, constellation hidden on cards, wrong sets cost 1). Laying a constellation down tells one culture's myth; tapping a card shows its real fact; the table draws each set as its stick figure. Oracle AI in `engine.js` (generator, so each step animates). ✦ = score × level; 💎 for wins, first wins and completed constellations. 📖 Study guide (`guide.js`). Sound (`sfx.js`, Web Audio, follows mute): shuffle, deal, flick, star chords, buzz, fanfare. Store shelf "🌌 Star cards": 7 card backs (`prog.rummyback`). Tests: `tests/rummy.test.js`.

- `/celtic`: Graves' tree calendar with ogham letter and fire-festival season. Labelled as a modern reconstruction.

## Cast systems (milestone 2)
- `/runes`: 24 Elder Futhark runes drawn without replacement; one, three (Norns) or five (cross) spreads; reversible runes land merkstave half the time.

### Rooms: History of Magic

**History of Magic** (`activity/src/timeline/`) is a timeline game: the Oracle deals real, datable events from the history of magic, stage conjuring, science and skepticism (65 cards across four eras: the Ancient World, Middle Ages & Renaissance, Age of Reason & Wonders, the Modern Age), and the player taps the gap on the timeline where each one belongs. The first card is laid down free; each wrong place empties one of 3 hourglasses and the card is filed where it really goes, with its date and story. Three levels: **1 Apprentice**, 5 cards across all history; **2 Adept**, 6 cards from an era and its neighbour; **3 Master**, 7 cards from one era, some only a few years apart. Cards are only dealt together when their dates, including the uncertainty of ancient ones, can't overlap (`events.js` `pm`), so every order is certain. Points 10 / 15 / 20 per card, plus leftover hourglasses and a perfect-game bonus. Each card placed right for the first time enters your Chronicle (Codex section by era) and pays 4 ✦ and 4 💎; a new best pays the improvement in ✦; a first perfect game at a level pays 10 / 20 / 30 💎. Titles: Time Tourist, Apprentice Chronicler, Keeper of Years, Royal Historian, Master of the Chronicle. Each era has a 📖 study guide (`guide.js`) covering every card in it. Store shelf ⏳ Timeline (`shop.js`): Parchment scroll (free), Astronomer's star chart, Inventor's blueprint, Illuminated manuscript, Time-machine neon. Doesn't use daily readings.

### Rooms: Alchemist's Lab

**Alchemist's Lab** (`activity/src/alchemy/`): mix real historical alchemy and learn the chemistry behind it. Three levels, 31 works: **1 Apprentice**, read the classic symbols (14: the seven planetary metals, sulfur, salt, the four elements, antimony); **2 Journeyman**, follow real recipes at the furnace (10: vermilion, lead calx, aqua fortis, aqua regia, dissolving gold, iron into "copper", sugar of lead, spirit of salt, aqua vitae, the Tree of Diana); **3 Adept**, repeat the experiments that made chemistry (7: Brand's phosphorus, Black's fixed air, Cavendish's hydrogen, Priestley's oxygen, Lavoisier's 12-day mercury experiment, Scheele's chlorine, Davy's potassium), then name what was really found. On the bench, pick up to 3 reagents and an operation (heat, dissolve, distil, sublime; level 3 adds electrolyse) and Transmute; feedback counts right, extra and missing reagents. Four furnace flames: a wrong answer, a failed mix or a notebook hint puts one out. Every result shows the history, the modern chemistry and a balanced equation (tests check every equation balances), and lights its elements on **My periodic table** (23 elements in their real places). Points ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2; first success pays ✦ and 💎, replays only improvements. Titles Puffer, Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Chymist. 📓 **Alchemist's notebook** (`guide.js`) is the study guide: history, elements and principles, metals and planets, operations, old names vs modern chemicals, alchemy to chemistry, safety. 🏺 **Apothecary**: 13 cosmetic bench items (vessels, flame-test coloured fires, wall charms) sold in-room and on the "⚗️ Alchemist's Lab" shelf of the Essence store (`shop.js`, registered with `registerShelf`). Content in `lab.js`, rules in `logic.js`, drawing in `scene.js`.
