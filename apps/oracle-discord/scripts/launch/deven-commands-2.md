**🃏 Draws and casts** (each takes an optional `question`)
• `/tarot`: one card, three (past, present, future), five (the crossroads) or the full Celtic cross.
• `/lenormand`: the 36-card deck read in pairs; two cards, a three-card sentence or the nine-card square.
• `/magicdeck`: our own 44-card deck, where every card carries a real science fact. Spreads run from a quick experiment to a five-card lab report.
• `/runes`: Elder Futhark from the bag; Odin's rune, the three Norns or the five-rune cross.
• `/iching`: flip six Science is Magic coins for a hexagram, with the classic odds kept intact.
• `/oraclebones`: write a charge ("The new job will go well"), crack a turtle plastron or ox shoulder blade, and read the omen the Shang way.
• `/ifa`: an Ifá-inspired reflection; cast the chain and see which of the 256 Odù appears.

Every reading follows one rule: the data first, then what it *may* mean, then open questions. No verdicts and no advice, but the jokes stay.

**📅 The daily sky and the daily boards** (the same for everyone each day)
• `/calendar` shows today, or any date, in the Maya, Egyptian and Celtic calendars, plus the next festival and the next eclipse.
• `/tonight city:` shows the real sky over your nearest big city: planets, Moon, bright stars, meteors and the Night Watch target. We only ever ask for a city.
• `/commission` posts today's three Commissions from the Alchemist's House: real clients asking for real products you make at the Discovery Bench.
• `/booking` posts Tonight's Bill from the Séance Parlour: today's three bookings for Madame Rook's clients.
