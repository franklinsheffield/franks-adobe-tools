# 🌍 Level 6 reward: Pixel Scape

Reach **level 6** and the 🧪 Pixel Scape channels open: build 3D worlds together, right here in Discord, on your computer or your phone. Founding Members already have it.

**🧱 `/pixelscape`** opens it in the channel or your voice call. Up to 8 of you build in the same scene, live.
- Start from a real place, a blank world, a library scene, or the build your friends already started.
- **Make** brings in real places, real buildings, your own photos (straight from your phone camera too) and 3D models.
- **Explore** hides the panels so you can fly or walk through what you built. Tap a name to follow a friend, long-press the ground to ping a spot.

**👷 Surveyor Flint** is your guide on the build site. He pops in to comment on (and roast) what you build. Tap him or press `?` to ask anything, and he'll show you where it is. Turn on **Teach mode** and tap any button to see what it does before you use it.

**🏔 `/terrain place:Grand Canyon`**: real elevation and satellite imagery, turned into a render and a .glb model in 1 to 3 minutes. **Open in Pixel Scape** to build on it together.

**📦 `/model find query:castle`**: about 6,800 free 3D models with credits. **Add to my scene** drops one into your build.

**🗓 Weekly build theme** in the room bar. **Post render** tags your shot, and every post has a **Remix** button.
