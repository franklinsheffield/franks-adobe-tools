# 🚪 The Houses are open

Science is Magic has five Houses now, and every one of them is a game.

🔮 **The Oracle Chamber** with the Oracle: readings, card games and the history of real magic
⚗️ **The Alchemist's House** with Magister Vell: flame tests, minerals and real chemistry
🔭 **The Observatory** with Keeper Altair: comets, eclipses, constellations and the real sky tonight
🕯️ **The Séance Parlour** with Madame Rook: case files, stage tricks and how the mediums did it
🪶 **The Scriptorium** with Archivist Wren: codes, ciphers, forgeries and the Bombe

**How it works**
Every game you play earns ✦, and ✦ raises your **Server level**. Each level opens more of the server:
• **Level 1:** pick your home House at the Five Doors in <#1576066769657798767>
• **Level 2:** a second House and your **class** (Astrochemist, Mentalist, Almanac Maker…) with its own Guild Hall
• **Level 3:** a third House and your **subclass**
• **Level 4:** the fourth door won't open… but **the Common Grounds** do, where every House meets
• **Levels 5 and 6:** your last two Houses, your Order, and the **Path Rite** that seals your path. Pixel Scape opens at level 6.
• **Levels 7 to 10:** Guide, Curator, Councillor, Magister. You help run the place.
Nothing is on a timer, and nothing can be bought that wins. Play is the only way up.

**The Halls** stay open for everyone to read. The Houses you open let you post in them, and the Scriptorium opens every Hall from day one.

**Already here? You start at level 4.** You get a Founding Member role, the Common Grounds from day one, and you keep Pixel Scape as it is today. Pick your home House and two more, and your class and subclass are yours. Each House you pick also sends a Founding gift: 150 💎 Essence and a purse of that House's tokens to spend on its relics.

**Coming soon:** House duels and the House Cup. The winning House holds the **Conch of Dignity** for a quarter. 🐚
