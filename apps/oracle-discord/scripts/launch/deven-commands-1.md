📜 *A second letter, Deven, sealed with the same wax.*

The first letter told you where the Houses stand. This one is your field manual: every slash command the server answers to, and what each one really does. Type `/` in any channel to see them, and pick the one with the Science is Magic icon.

A few things hold for every reading command:
• Dates are always **DD/MM/YYYY**.
• `private: True` means only you see the reply. Most readings post to the channel unless you set it.
• Save your birth details once with `/profile set` and every birth-data command fills them in for you. Birth data is encrypted and never logged.

**🚪 The doors**
• `/play` opens the House of the channel you're in: its host, games and store. One app serves all five Houses, so the same command gives you Vell in the Alchemist's channel and Wren in the Scriptorium.
• `/oracle` is the guided route: pick a system, give your details, get a reading card.
• `/profile set | show | delete` saves, shows (only to you) or wipes your birth date, place, time and full birth name.

**🌌 Charts from your birth date**
• `/astrology chart` draws your natal wheel and reading (Whole Sign or Placidus houses; private by default).
• `/astrology now` shows the sky right now, and with `my_chart` or your birth details, how it touches your chart.
• `/humandesign` draws your bodygraph from real planetary positions.
• `/numerology` gives your Pythagorean core numbers from full birth name and date.
• `/chinesezodiac` gives your animal, element and four pillars from the real lunar calendar.
• `/mayan` gives your Tzolk'in day sign and tone, Haab' date and Long Count.
• `/egyptian` gives your Egyptian zodiac deity and your birthday in the Egyptian calendar.
• `/celtic` gives your tree sign and ogham letter.
The Egyptian and Celtic signs are modern reconstructions, and the cards say so.
