# Frank's Mobglow Brew 2.0

One-click realistic, dreamy glow: lights spill soft, coloured haze over the untouched original. (UXP panel, Photoshop 24.4+.)

Install: double-click `dist/mobglow-2.0.0.ccx`, then open Plugins > Frank's Mobglow Brew.

1. Pick a look in the Look browser or Library (Dreamy, Fog City, Neon Rain, Soft Portrait, Hot Lights, Blue Hour, Rose Haze, Neon Edges).
2. Glow card: Radius (how far light spreads, px on a 2048 px long side), Strength, Threshold (relative to the picture's brightest lights), Gamma (below 1 pulls mid-tones into the haze), Saturation, and Color (tint the glow one colour).
   - Image key: glow from the flattened image or the active layer.
   - Eyedropper key: click it, then click the preview to take the glow colour from the picture.
   - Brush key: after a Render, adds a mask to the Glow layer and picks the Brush tool (black paints glow away).
3. Before / After (under the preview) splits the preview; drag on it to move the line.
4. Render. A new group "Frank's Mobglow Brew - <look>" holds the Glow layer (Screen) over your image, plus Background darken if used. One Cmd+Z undoes it.
